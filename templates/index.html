Recover Original Dataset?

This will permanently remove all uploaded datasets and restore VergeMap to its original base dataset. The original dataset covers air quality and environmental data from 2014–2023 and contains 2,250 records. All imported datasets and their associated data will be permanently deleted. This action cannot be undone.

  • ⬤ All records have a Year of 2024 or later

  • ⬤ Numeric columns contain valid numbers (AQI, PM2.5, PM10, etc.)
  • ⬤ AQI values are within a plausible range (0 – 500)
